## Why this journal

The most advanced assistive technologies, such as **Brain Computer Interfaces**, often stay
locked in laboratories or are sold at unreachable prices. Yet for people living with a severe
motor disability, they can make the difference between depending on someone and moving on their own.

I chose to do research **in the open**: instead of waiting for a final result to publish,
I describe the work here while I am doing it. Anyone can follow it, check it,
reuse it or help me improve it.
