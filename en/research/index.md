The research lines share the same goal: giving independence back to people living with a
motor disability. **SmartChair** is about movement, with a powered wheelchair driven by
brainwaves; **Kite** is about communication, with an Augmentative and Alternative Communication
tool. Each one is a project of its own, with its own open materials and journal.
