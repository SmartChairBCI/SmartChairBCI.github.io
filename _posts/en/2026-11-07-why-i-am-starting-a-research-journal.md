Research usually only becomes visible at the end, in a scientific paper that describes
the result and almost never the road that led to it. I decided to do things differently.

## What you will find here

Every journal entry has a **type**:

- **Update**: where a research line stands.
- **Experiment**: what I tried, how and why.
- **Result**: what I measured, with the data available to everyone.
- **Note**: notes, readings, reasoning.
- **Explainer**: explanations for non-specialists.

Almost every entry is linked to one of my [research lines](/en/research/). Each line has a
page showing its current phase and next steps.

## Why in public

> Open science is not just publishing the code: it is making what you do understandable.

Working in public forces me to be clear and lets others correct me early.
If you spot a mistake, [write to me](/en/contact/).
